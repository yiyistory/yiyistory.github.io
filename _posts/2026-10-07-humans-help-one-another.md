<div class="poem">
<p>She buys the seedlings and tosses them into the soil<br>
He drives the machine to harvest the rice<br>
He pays with one hand and takes the goods with the other<br>
She cooks it and sets it on my dinner table</p>
<p>She raises it until it is grown<br>
He cuts open its hide<br>
She makes the hide into shoes<br>
I buy them in a store</p>
<p>A typhoon sweeps the city with rain<br>
The power plant in the suburbs blazes with light<br>
The cars vanish from the roads<br>
I am at home playing on my phone</p>
<p>Hurry, hurry, time is almost up<br>
Hurry, hurry, my hungry stomach is growling<br>
Hurry, hurry, I'm already at the door<br>
Okay, okay, thank you, goodbye</p>
<p>Hello, here's what you ordered<br>
Thanks, here's the money<br>
We help one another<br>
We owe one another nothing</p>
</div>
