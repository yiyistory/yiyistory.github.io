<div class="poem">
<p>Shouting glorious slogans, they seize the labor of others. The game of emperors has played out for a thousand years, and another thousand years.<br>
What is the fastest way to make money? Not being a skilled craftsman, not having a silver tongue, not being a brilliant actor, not shedding blood and sweat.<br>
It is becoming someone else's king.</p>
<p>This world could have been at peace long ago; the grain we grow could have fed everyone long ago.<br>
But the kings are insatiable, and the kings fear you will slack off.<br>
The kings also fight one another for territory.<br>
The kings teach people to hate, the kings promise a future that does not exist.<br>
One group of kings launches a righteous war, another group of kings leads the people in righteous resistance.</p>
<p>Anyway, whoever wins is righteous, and if you lose, it is only death, so what<br>
A life lasts only a few decades; just tell a lie and gamble it all</p>
