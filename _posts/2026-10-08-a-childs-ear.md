<div class="poem">
<p>A child's ear<br>
Like the black hole at the center of a newborn universe<br>
Greedily swallowing the vibrations of the world<br>
Stars tremble and pour down light like rain<br>
Leaves clash, layer on layer like waves<br>
A mother's lips murmur and call</p>
