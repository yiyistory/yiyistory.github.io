<div class="poem">
<p>Child, it must have hurt so much, didn't it?<br>
The bombs that fell from the sky<br>
The broken home<br>
Your father's weeping face, covered in dust<br>
And your mother, eyes open, no longer answering<br>
Your small body, it must have hurt so much</p>

<p>I am sorry<br>
Among the grown-ups there are such demons<br>
They just dropped bombs onto your home<br>
They just killed your family and your friends<br>
They just destroyed the world you had not yet had the chance to see</p>

<p>Child, I am sorry<br>
I am only an ordinary person, mountains and seas away from you<br>
But when that shell fell beside you<br>
Every one of us bore responsibility<br>
It was our silence that let the evil of the world go on</p>

<p>Child, rest in peace<br>
Don't look at me like that, please, rest in peace!</p>
</div>
