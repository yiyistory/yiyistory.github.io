<p>Is the love I feel really love, or a reaction of chemicals deep in my brain<br>
Is the dignity I feel really dignity, or some hormone flowing through my body<br>
Is my happiness really happiness, where does my sadness truly come from<br>
When some mixed liquid fills the hollow of my heart, I feel hatred<br>
When that liquid slowly evaporates, I can no longer hold on to the hate<br>
When the sensation that substance brings fades from my brain, I name it forgiveness<br>
I have not forgiven the world; my body is simply not built to sustain a lasting hatred</p>
<p>True or false may not matter; I sense that beyond my feelings there is still a me<br>
A me who gets hungry and cold, a me who slacks off and dreams, a me who craves vegetables after too much meat<br>
A me who can never stick with anything, a me who wants to run but never bought running shoes<br>
A me whose mind sometimes goes blank, a me who starts thinking and halfway through wants to make money<br>
A me who wants to do great things, and is too lazy to get out of bed<br>
A me who wants to know the origin of humankind, a me who thinks about the depths of the universe<br>
A me who wants to know when humans became human, a me who wants to know what lies beyond human senses<br>
A me who wanders freely through human knowledge, a me who fell in love with Wong Ka Kui's music<br>
A me who at thirty-five suddenly thought: do not fail to do good because it is small, do not do evil because it is small<br>
A me who sleeps and wakes and sleeps again, weary and hopeful<br>
A me who wants to overcome everything, a me in whom strength is born</p>
