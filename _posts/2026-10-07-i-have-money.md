---
title: I Have Money

excerpt: I have one yuan
---

<div class="poem">
<p>I have one yuan<br>
I buy candy at the store<br>
I get scolded when I go home</p>
<p>I have ten yuan<br>
I stay up all night at the internet café<br>
I get a beating when I go home</p>
<p>I have a hundred yuan<br>
I go out for a late-night meal<br>
I'm not going home tonight</p>
<p>I have a thousand yuan<br>
After the rent there isn't much left<br>
I don't dare go home for the New Year</p>
<p>I have ten thousand yuan<br>
I fall in love<br>
I call home</p>
<p>I have a hundred thousand yuan<br>
I scrape it together to get married<br>
I call Mom and Dad again</p>
<p>I have a million yuan<br>
I borrow two million more to buy a house<br>
I have a home of my own</p>
<p>I have ten million yuan<br>
All right, all right<br>
Now I have everything</p>
<p>I have a lot of money<br>
I am old now<br>
I no longer have a mom and dad</p>
</div>
