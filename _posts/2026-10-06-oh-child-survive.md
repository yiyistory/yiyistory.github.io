title: Oh Child, Survive

<div class="poem">
<p>Tank treads roll through a mud pit<br>
A child's bicycle wheels roll through a puddle<br>
Where a child's face is reflected<br>
The gray sky behind him<br>
Is history's falling shells<br>
Someone wants to blow him to pieces<br>
The blue sky behind him<br>
Is high-rises clad in glass<br>
The children who were not blown to pieces<br>
Grew up and built these towers to protect him</p>
<p>Who has seen black planes sweep across the sky<br>
Who has seen shells fall on buildings at lunchtime<br>
Who has seen bayonets slit open the bellies of the innocent<br>
Who has seen children sitting beside their dead parents</p>
<p>Sadly, the onlookers never pity the weak<br>
People just stand with the strong and enjoy the thrill of justice</p>
<p>If the child does not survive<br>
If the whole people walks into extinction<br>
People will say it was the price of progress<br>
People will say the dead did great evil while they lived<br>
People will say there is no building without breaking<br>
People will say anything, as if they had won a victory</p>
<p>Interests calculated, gains and losses weighed<br>
Borders drawn on land divide conscience from conscience<br>
Plain love loses to two kinds of stern righteousness<br>
In the crowd I open my mouth and no sound comes out</p>
<p>Oh child, survive<br>
Only if you survive can you carry justice into the future<br>
Only if you survive can you grow up and shut the mouths of those who speak for you<br>
Oh child, please survive</p>
</div>
