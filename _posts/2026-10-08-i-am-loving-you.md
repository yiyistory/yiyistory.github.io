<div class="poem">
<p>Hello<br>
I am loving you</p>
<p>You are ugly<br>
I am loving you<br>
You are beautiful<br>
I am loving you</p>
<p>You are tall<br>
I am loving you<br>
You are short<br>
I am loving you<br>
You are neither tall nor short<br>
I am still loving you</p>
<p>You just finished cooking<br>
I am loving you<br>
You just finished digging coal<br>
I am loving you<br>
You deliver takeout<br>
You deliver packages<br>
You toss your head in a bar<br>
I am loving, loving you all the same</p>
<p>You are honest<br>
I am loving you<br>
You have told lies<br>
I am loving you<br>
You risked your life to save someone<br>
I am loving you<br>
You killed someone<br>
I am loving you</p>
<p>You always come first in exams<br>
I am loving you<br>
You are dumb as a pig<br>
I am loving you<br>
You were born lacking something<br>
I'm sorry, I am loving you</p>
<p>Now, think of nothing<br>
In a faraway place<br>
In a faraway time<br>
Someone is telling you<br>
I am loving you</p>
</div>
