<div class="poem">
<p>School taught us to protect the environment<br>
Every lesson in our textbooks spoke of love and peace</p>

<p>Yet the grown-ups are all destroying the environment<br>
The fires of war have never gone out in this world, devouring life after life</p>

<p>Only when I grew up did I see through it<br>
It was all for money</p>

<p>Pouring black water into clear rivers<br>
Is to save the cost of treating sewage</p>

<p>Firing bullets into other people's heads<br>
Is so the living will obey and make money for them</p>

<p>But what a bunch of losers they must be<br>
Can't make money without polluting?<br>
Can't make money without war?</p>
</div>
