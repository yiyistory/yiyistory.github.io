<div class="poem">
<p>The sky presses down<br>
Falling upon every one of us<br>
The river of stars flows into every body</p>

<p>Put the world into us<br>
Put us into the world<br>
It is all the same<br>
Because every atom of our bodies<br>
Came from stardust to begin with</p>
</div>
